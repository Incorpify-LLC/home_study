# Curriculum sources

_Last reviewed: 20 October 2026_

This register separates the authoritative curriculum scope from supplementary teaching references. Source documents are research inputs only. Home Study publishes original explanations, examples, and questions; it does not reproduce textbook passages or question banks.

## Authority and scope

### Class VIII Mathematics

**Authoritative curriculum framework**

- Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE), _Curriculum for Upper Primary Classes (VI–VIII)_, first edition November 2016.[1]
- Official URL: <https://cisce.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/UpperPrimary.pdf>
- The document states that CISCE does not prescribe textbooks for Preschool–Class VIII and permits schools to choose suitable books.
- This is an official CISCE curriculum document, but it is not a “2027 Class VIII board syllabus.” Schools may contextualise it, so the school’s current chapter order remains the best source for sequencing.

The Class VIII Mathematics scope is organised into six themes:

1. **Number System** — rational-number properties and representation; integral exponents; squares, square roots, cubes and cube roots; number puzzles and divisibility; union, intersection, disjoint sets, and complements.
2. **Ratio and Proportion** — percentage, profit and loss, overheads, discount and tax; simple and compound interest; direct and inverse variation; time and work.
3. **Algebra** — multiplication and division of algebraic expressions; identities; factorisation; linear equations in one variable; inequalities in one variable.
4. **Geometry** — quadrilateral and parallelogram properties; 2-D representations of 3-D shapes; Euler’s relation; quadrilateral construction; reflection symmetry; parts of a circle; elementary Cartesian graphs.
5. **Mensuration** — areas of trapezia, polygons and semicircles; surface areas of cubes, cuboids and cylinders; volumes and capacities of cuboids and cylinders.
6. **Data Handling** — grouped data and bar graphs; pie charts; experimental ideas of chance and probability.

**Pedagogy source**

- CISCE, _Mathematics at the Upper Primary Level_.[2]
- Official URL: <https://cisce.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Module-II-Mathematics-Upper.pdf>
- Useful for activity-led teaching, prerequisite checks, misconception analysis, and continuous assessment.

### Class X Mathematics — ICSE Examination Year 2027

**Authoritative syllabus**

- CISCE, _ICSE Mathematics (51), Examination Year 2027_, February 2025.[3]
- Official URL: <https://cisce.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/9.-Mathematics.pdf>
- Verified in the document itself as “YEAR 2027” and “ICSE Examination Year 2027.”
- One three-hour paper carries 80 marks, with 20 marks for internal assessment.

The Class X scope comprises:

1. **Commercial Mathematics** — GST; recurring deposits; shares and dividends.
2. **Algebra** — linear inequations; quadratic equations; ratio and proportion; factor and remainder theorems; matrices; arithmetic and geometric progressions; coordinate geometry.
3. **Geometry** — similarity; loci; circle theorems; constructions.
4. **Mensuration** — surface area and volume of cylinders, cones, spheres, and combinations of solids; melting and recasting.
5. **Trigonometry** — identities; heights and distances.
6. **Statistics** — measures of central tendency for raw and grouped data; histograms; less-than ogives; quartiles and interquartile range.
7. **Probability** — sample spaces, events, and simple single-event problems.

### Class XII Accountancy — ISC Examination Year 2027

**Authoritative syllabus**

- CISCE, _ISC Accountancy (858), Examination Year 2027_, February 2025.[4]
- Official URL: <https://cisce.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/15.-ISC-Accountancy.docx.pdf>
- Verified in the document itself as “YEAR 2027” and “ISC Examination Year 2027.”
- Paper I is a three-hour theory paper carrying 80 marks; Paper II is project work carrying 20 marks.

Paper I has compulsory Section A (60 marks) and a choice between Section B and Section C (20 marks):

- **Section A — Partnership Accounts and Joint Stock Company Accounts**
  - Partnership fundamentals, goodwill, admission, retirement/death, dissolution.
  - Issue of shares, issue and redemption of debentures, and final accounts of companies.
- **Section B — Management Accounting**
  - Financial statement analysis, cash-flow statements, and ratio analysis.
- **Section C — Computerised Accounting**
  - Accounting applications of spreadsheets and database management systems.

The initial Home Study Accountancy track uses Section B, not Section C, unless the learner’s school confirms a different option.

### Class VIII Physics — CISCE Upper Primary

**Authoritative curriculum framework**

- Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE), _Curriculum for Upper Primary Classes (VI–VIII)_, first edition November 2016.[1]
- Official URL: <https://cisce.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/UpperPrimary.pdf>
- Same caveat as Class VIII Mathematics: this is an official CISCE curriculum framework, not a 2027 board examination syllabus, so the school’s current chapter order still controls sequencing.

The Class VIII Physics scope derived from this document covers:

1. **Matter and the kinetic theory** — three states, particle movement and energy, boiling, vaporisation, melting, fusion, evaporation, condensation, sublimation, deposition, freezing.
2. **Measurement** — density of irregular solids by displacement (Eureka can, measuring cylinder), density of fluids, floatation and sinking via relative density.
3. **Force and pressure** — turning effect and moment of force, pressure and its units, pressure in liquids and gases, atmospheric pressure (qualitative).
4. **Energy** — work, kinetic energy, potential and gravitational potential energy (g = 10 m/s²), energy transformation, energy versus power.
5. **Light energy** — refraction, concave and convex mirrors with ray rules, real versus virtual images, dispersion by a prism.
6. **Heat** — boiling versus evaporation, linear/superficial/volume thermal expansion, comparative expansivity of solids, liquids and gases.
7. **Sound** — pitch and frequency, instruments (string, membrane, wind), monotone, loudness and amplitude, decibel.
8. **Electricity** — household consumption in kilowatt-hour, live/neutral/earth wiring, fuses and circuit breakers, static electricity, conduction and induction, electroscope, lightning conductor, electrical safety.

### Class X Physics — ICSE Examination Year 2027

**Authoritative syllabus**

- CISCE, _ICSE Science (52) Physics, Examination Year 2027_, February 2025.[10]
- Official URL: <https://cisce.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/10.-Physics.pdf>
- Science Paper-1; the document prints a Class IX section (Measurements and Experimentation, Motion in One Dimension, Laws of Motion, Fluids, Heat, Light, Sound, Electricity and Magnetism) followed by the Class X section used here.
- One two-hour paper carries 80 marks, with 20 marks for internal assessment of practical work. Only SI units are to be used.

The Class X scope comprises six units:

1. **Force, Work, Power and Energy** — turning forces and moment of a force, centre of gravity, translational and rotational equilibrium, uniform circular motion, work with special cases, potential and kinetic energy, energy conversion, power, machines as force multipliers (levers, pulleys, mechanical advantage, velocity ratio, efficiency).
2. **Light** — refraction and its laws, refractive index, real and apparent depth, total internal reflection and its applications, converging and diverging lenses and image formation by ray diagrams, magnification, dispersion through a prism, electromagnetic spectrum, scattering.
3. **Sound** — nature and propagation of sound waves, V = fλ, echoes and SONAR, natural, damped and forced vibrations, resonance, loudness and intensity, decibel, pitch and quality, noise pollution.
4. **Electricity and Magnetism** — Ohm’s law, emf and potential difference, resistance and its factors, series and parallel combinations, internal resistance, electrical power and energy, household circuits and wiring safety, magnetic effect of current, Oersted’s experiment, electromagnets, Fleming’s left-hand rule, DC motor, electromagnetic induction, AC generator, transformer.
5. **Heat** — calorimetry, specific heat capacity and thermal capacity, method of mixtures, latent heat of fusion, heating curve for water.
6. **Modern Physics** — radioactivity and nuclear changes, α, β and γ radiations, decay equations, radio isotopes and safety precautions, background radiation, nuclear fission and fusion.

### Class XII Commerce — ISC Examination Year 2027

**Authoritative syllabus**

- CISCE, _ISC Commerce (857), Examination Year 2027_, February 2025.[11]
- Official URL: <https://cisce.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/14.-ISC-Commerce.doc.pdf>
- Paper I (Theory) carries 80 marks with four units — Business Environment (7), Financing (20), Management (33), Marketing (20) — plus project work carrying 20 marks.

The Class XII scope comprises:

1. **Business Environment** — concept, features and importance; environmental scanning; micro (internal and external) and macro (economic, social, technological, political, legal) dimensions; SWOT analysis.
2. **Financing** — sources of finance for sole trader, partnership and company; financial planning; capital structure, fixed capital and working capital; equity and preference shares, bonus and rights shares, ESOP, sweat equity, retained earnings; debentures and other loan capital; bank and institutional lending; short-term sources including public deposits, trade credit, factoring, inter-corporate deposits and instalment credit; modern banking services (RTGS, NEFT, IMPS, e-banking, mobile banking, UPI, e-wallet).
3. **Management** — meaning, nature, objectives and levels; Taylor’s scientific management and Fayol’s principles; planning, organising and staffing; directing, leadership and communication; controlling and coordination.
4. **Marketing** — meaning and types of markets; traditional versus modern marketing concepts; marketing functions; the marketing mix of product, price, place and promotion; consumer protection and the Consumer Protection Act 2019.

## Reviewed learning releases

Every chapter now publishes an original lesson, worked example, and diagnostic; chapter progression is sequential and independent per subject, and a learner’s roadmap shows all published chapters with completion state.

**First release — Mathematics and Accountancy**

- **Class VIII Mathematics (CISCE-aligned, 11 chapters):** rational numbers and integral exponents; squares, square roots, cubes, cube roots, number puzzles and sets; percentages, profit, loss, overheads, discount and tax; simple and compound interest; direct and inverse variation and time and work; algebraic expressions, identities and factorisation; linear equations and inequalities; quadrilaterals, parallelograms and Euler's relation; construction, reflection symmetry, circles and Cartesian graphs; mensuration; grouped data, pie charts and experimental probability.
- **Class X Mathematics (ICSE 2027, 18 chapters):** the full Commercial Mathematics, Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Trigonometry, Statistics, and Probability scope.
- **Class XII Accountancy (ISC 2027, 12 chapters):** the full Section A partnership and company accounts scope plus the Section B Management Accounting option.

**Second release — Physics and Commerce**

- **Class VIII Physics (CISCE Upper Primary, 8 chapters):** matter and kinetic theory; density and floatation; moment of force and pressure; work, energy and power; refraction, curved mirrors and dispersion; heat transfer and thermal expansion; sound, pitch and loudness; household electricity, static electricity and safety.
- **Class X Physics (ICSE 2027, 10 chapters):** the full Force/Work/Power/Energy, Light, Sound, Electricity and Magnetism, Heat and Modern Physics scope.
- **Class XII Commerce (ISC 2027, 12 chapters):** the full Business Environment, Financing, Management, and Marketing scope including the Consumer Protection Act 2019.

Content batches were independently reviewed against the official CISCE documents. First-release review findings (a defective cryptarithm, an out-of-scope square-root decimal, missing modal-class and non-axial reflection coverage, a garbled cash-flow step) were corrected before publication. Rough-work upload/review, reassessment, adaptation, and email reports remain future slices.

## Review rules

1. Every student-visible topic, lesson, and question must be marked `reviewed` before the API returns it.
2. Every curriculum record must link to at least one source record with publisher, URL, retrieval date, and scope note.
3. Questions and worked examples must be original. Numbers, names, and contexts must not be lifted from copyrighted exercises.
4. Source material must remain outside Git unless its licence explicitly permits redistribution.
5. Class VIII must be labelled as the CISCE Upper Primary curriculum, not as a 2027 board examination syllabus.
6. School-prescribed books or chapter lists, when supplied, control sequencing but do not override the official Class X/XII examination scope.
7. A class may carry more than one subject track. Each subject keeps its own chapter sequence and progress state; completing a chapter in one subject must never unlock a chapter in another.
